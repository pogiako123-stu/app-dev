# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is my favorite series. It is a science fiction and mystery series about a group of friends who experience strange events in their town.

### Why I Like This Series

- The story is interesting.
- The characters are fun to watch.
- It has exciting and surprising moments.
- The friendship between the characters is inspiring.
